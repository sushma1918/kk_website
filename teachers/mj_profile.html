DR.MUMTAJ ANSARI (MJ Sir)

Science Faculty - Chemistry & Biology

Over 15 Years Experience in CBSE/UP/ICSE Boards

Greetings to all my readers and viewers.

Since my primary education, I have been interested in Science. I completed my primary, secondary, and senior secondary education in Hindi medium from my state board. I have more than 15 years of experience teaching students from CBSE, UP, and ICSE boards, specializing in Chemistry and Biology.

For comprehensive student growth, it’s essential to balance board syllabus and competitive preparation. My students are practical and logical thinkers. I aim to provide them with foundational knowledge because, without a solid foundation, we cannot build a sky-high tower. Similarly, without fundamental knowledge, cracking competitive exams is challenging.

I encourage students to value practical knowledge over theoretical learning. Books are the bricks of a successful future.

“There is no shortcut to complete knowledge.”

Thank you to all,

MJ Sir
